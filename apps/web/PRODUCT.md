# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Voira is used by people who think more clearly out loud than on paper and need to turn spoken thoughts into polished written communication:

- Applicants writing essays and personal statements (students, scholarship seekers).
- Interview candidates practicing and refining spoken answers.
- Early-career professionals converting speech into structured, confident prose.

Their context is high-stakes and time-pressured: a deadline, an application, an interview. They arrive wanting momentum, not a blank page. On the marketing surface they are evaluating whether Voira is credible, premium, and worth their trust before they speak a word.

## Product Purpose

Voira is a voice-first communication coaching app. Users speak their answer to a prompt; Voira transcribes live (Deepgram) and an LLM (OpenAI) refines the transcript into a polished draft that still sounds like them. Success is a user moving from raw spoken thought to a clear, confident written draft they'd actually submit, without losing their own voice.

The landing page's job is to make that promise feel inevitable and premium: "say it out loud, we'll make it read beautifully."

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, premium, aspirational. Voira should feel like a high-craft creative tool, not a utility. The voice is calm and self-assured, never salesy or hype-driven. Typography and motion do the emotional work; the interface breathes. Three words: cinematic, premium, aspirational.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates: Inter for everything, purple-to-blue gradients, identical icon-heading-text card grids, hero-metric templates.
- Cluttered, dense, information-overload layouts that overwhelm before they explain.
- The typical "AI tool" look: glowing orbs scattered everywhere, dark-mode-with-neon, gimmicky sci-fi framing.

## Design Principles

- **Show the transformation, don't describe it.** Let motion, typography, and real product moments demonstrate speech-to-refined-writing rather than listing features.
- **Cinematic restraint.** Depth and drama come from a few committed elements (video, serif display type, glass) rather than many competing effects. Remove before adding.
- **Sound like a human, read like a pro.** Copy is calm and confident; it never shouts or over-promises.
- **Every surface earns its motion.** Animation is intentional and reveals meaning, with a reduced-motion path always available.
- **Trust is the conversion.** Premium craft signals that the output will be premium too.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text >=4.5:1 contrast, large/display text >=3:1, including over the video background (use scrims/overlays where needed so text never relies on a specific video frame for contrast).
- Full `prefers-reduced-motion` support: fade-rise, parallax, and looping video all need a reduced/paused alternative.
- Keyboard-navigable nav and CTAs with visible focus states; background video is decorative (muted, `aria-hidden`) and never the sole carrier of meaning.
